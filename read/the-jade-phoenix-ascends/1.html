Chapter 1 · The Fallen Sword

The Jade Phoenix Ascends · by Ling Yue

The sword lay where Xu Wei had dropped it, half-buried in ash, its edge feathered with soot. Around her, the Jade Sect burned — not with fire, but with the strange, silent cold of a Celestial purge that left no smoke. The hall where she had knelt for twenty years was a skeleton of black beams, and the name she had carved into its pillar on her seventh birthday was gone.

She knelt, and her fingers closed around the hilt. The blade had been her father's, and before that her grandfather's — a plain iron sword with no spirit, no name, no legend. It was, the others had always said, beneath a sword-maiden of the Jade Sect. But she was no longer a sword-maiden of the Jade Sect. She was a traitor's daughter, and the only thing she had ever truly owned.

From the ruin, a voice — thin as a thread of gold — spoke directly into the turning of her heart. "You will not die here."

Xu Wei looked up. Above the burning hall, the sky had split into a wound, and through it, a single ember drifted down like a slow, falling star. It landed on the flat of her father's iron sword, and where it touched, the old black steel woke with a ring of jade. Light ran through the grain of the metal, and for the first time in her life, the sword had a name.

She whispered it, and the Nine Heavens, far above, seemed to turn to listen.
