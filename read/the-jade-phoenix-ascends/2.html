Chapter 2 · Ember of the Phoenix

The Jade Phoenix Ascends · by Ling Yue

The ember did not burn her hand. It sat in her palm like a small, patient heart, casting the ruined hall in a warm amber light, and Xu Wei understood that it had chosen her for a reason she was not yet allowed to know.

"The Celestial Emperor burned my kind to silence," the ember said — and now the voice had a shape, the shape of a woman bending low over the ashes. "Three thousand years ago, they clipped the wings of the phoenix and scattered its fires across the mortal world, so that no mortal would ever again rise. I am the last of those fires."

Xu Wei looked at the plain iron sword in her hand, now threaded with jade veins. "And you want me to carry you."

"No." The phoenix's voice grew close and fierce. "I want you to rise. Not as a sword-maiden of a burned sect. Not as a traitor's daughter. Rise as the one who carries the last ember, and make the heavens regret what they did to those who never had wings."

In the distance, cold trumpet-smoke coiled up from the valley — the Celestial soldiers, come to make sure nothing remained. Xu Wei turned, her father's sword singing in her grip, and the ember settled into the hollow of her collarbone like a second, burning heart.

She had no sect. She had no name. But she walked out of the ashes toward the army, and the first snow of the night began to fall.
