The Jade Phoenix Ascends

by Ling Yue
CultivationFantasyAdventure

When Xu Wei's sword is broken and her sect betrayed to the ashes, a dying phoenix-goddess grants her a single ember. To reclaim her name, Xu Wei must climb the Nine Heavens — past immortal courts, scheming elders, and a Celestial Emperor who would burn a mortal's defiance to cinders. Every spark she guards is a rebellion; every step upward, a war.
