The Complete Tale of Yue Fei

by Qian Cai & Jin Feng ·《说岳全传》
HistoricalEpicLoyalty

The theme of this epic carries forward the centuries-old ideal of loyalty to the sovereign and love of country that has endured since the Song dynasty. It warmly eulogizes Yue Fei and his loyal household army — their devotion to resisting invaders and defending their homeland — and ruthlessly condemns the shameless treachery of Qin Hui and other corrupt ministers who surrendered, sold out their nation, and hounded the loyal to death. The very essence of this thought is embodied in the figure of Yue Fei: fiercely brave, remarkably ingenious, loyal and filial, faithful and righteous, and utterly devoted to serving his country — an idealized hero. For the sake of a single word — loyalty — he would set aside victory or defeat on the battlefield, cast aside his own life and family, and meet death with unwavering resolve.
