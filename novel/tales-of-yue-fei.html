The Complete Tale of Yue Fei

by Qian Cai & Jin Feng ·《说岳全传》
HistoricalEpicLoyalty

The theme of this epic carries forward the centuries-old ideal of loyalty to the sovereign and love of country that has endured since the Song dynasty. It warmly eulogizes Yue Fei and his loyal household army — their devotion to resisting invaders and defending their homeland — and ruthlessly condemns the shameless treachery of Qin Hui and other corrupt ministers who surrendered, sold out their nation, and hounded the loyal to death. For the sake of a single word — loyalty — Yue Fei would set aside victory or defeat on the battlefield, cast aside his own life and family, and meet death with unwavering resolve. This is precisely what has moved countless readers for a thousand years. Yet from today's perspective, such steadfast loyalty, filial piety, and righteousness carry with them a great deal of what might be called "foolish loyalty" — a pity.

The authors, Qian Cai and Jin Feng, believed that in writing historical fiction, "if everything is invented it becomes absurd, and if everything is factual it becomes dull." One should "make the real seem unreal, and the unreal seem real" in order to capture readers. It is precisely this blend of fact and fiction that let the authors draw freely on the materials of oral storytelling, opera, and legend, and so weave out many brilliant episodes — such as "Spearing Little King Liang," "Yue Fei's Mother Tattoos Her Son," "Gao Chong Lifts the Rolling Cart," "Liang Hongyu Beats the Drum at Jinshan," "Wang Zuo Severs His Own Arm," and "Niu Gao Tears Up the Imperial Edict" — stories cherished by readers everywhere. As Jin Feng's preface says, the characterization highlights "Yue Fei's loyalty, Qin Hui's treachery, and Wuzhu's arrogance."

Yet the figure who leaves the deepest impression is Niu Gao. Upright, straightforward, and simple-hearted, fierce and reckless, he is a Li Kui–style outlaw hero who always turns calamity into good fortune and meets peril with a blessing — a lucky general. This uniquely distinctive character is beloved by all.
